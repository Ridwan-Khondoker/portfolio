About me

Half developer, half project manager, and that is the point.

I am Ridwan — Senior Developer and Project Manager at Tribe Marketing and Happy to Deliver. Most of my work sits where engineering meets delivery: scoping the build, writing the architecture, coding the hard parts, then keeping a distributed team moving until it is live.

Day to day that means Laravel and Vue on the platform side, Next.js on the marketing side, and the unglamorous work in between — database design, access control, QA plans, cost estimates, and the client call where all of it has to make sense in plain language.

I like projects that are slightly too big for the timeline. That is usually where the interesting architecture decisions live.

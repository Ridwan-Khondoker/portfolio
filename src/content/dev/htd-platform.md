Happy To Deliver receives packages on behalf of residential buildings and gets them to residents. The original product ran on a no-code app that had hit its ceiling: slow to change, hard to secure, and impossible to integrate with the property systems buildings already use.

I led the rebuild as a Laravel 12 monolith, owning the data model, the access rules and the release plan, and running the distributed team that shipped it. The result is one platform with a Filament admin for operations, a Vue 3 app for couriers in the field, and resident-facing notifications, all on a 49-table schema designed for the integrations that came next.
